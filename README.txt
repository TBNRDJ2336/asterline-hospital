Asterline Hospital website
Owner: Alen Mathew Thomas

How to open
- Unzip, then double-click index.html. It runs in any modern browser.
- It needs an internet connection the first time, because it loads the
  Three.js 3D library (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) and the fonts (Google Fonts).

How to customise
- Hospital name, departments, doctors, beds and the emergency queue are
  plain data near the bottom of index.html (search for DEPTS, DOCS, QUEUE).
- Colours and fonts are the variables at the top of the <style> block.
- The address and phone number in the footer are placeholders.
- The booking form is a demo and does not send anything anywhere.
